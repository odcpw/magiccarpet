# P5 preregistration: transfer contamination and receipt revocation

- Packet: `PKT-SYNTH-P5-0001`, schema 1
- Run the clean and contaminated known-source variants once each.
- The contaminated vector must fail acceptance and its transfer ends
  `PRIVATE_ONLY`.
- Self-review and inherited/raw-context review attempts must fail without
  changing the registry.
- The clean transfer requires an exact fresh independent review, becomes
  `APPROVED`, receives an exact current receipt, and becomes `CONSUMED`.
- Non-atomic revocation must fail; atomic receipt `STALE_REVOKED` plus transfer
  `REVOKED` must succeed.
- Scope: known-source synthetic protocol qualification only; no MC2 inference.

`P5.json` is the normative machine-readable packet.
